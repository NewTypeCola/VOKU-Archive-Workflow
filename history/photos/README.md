## Preparation for publication

All 18 photographs are WebP copies with a longest side of at most 1,600px, preserving the full original composition and aspect ratio. English filenames are used, and metadata including EXIF/XMP location, time, and device information was removed. Small blurs were applied only to two reflected faces in the recording-room glass and two background faces in the car photograph. Existing blur on the desk's screen and scripts was retained.

The root's [representative strip](../assets/photos/archive-process-strip.webp) is a 2,520×1,080px image joining five photographs—bookshelf, cart, car, living room, and desk—cropped to a common height. Individual photographs and the strip use [MIT-0](../../LICENSE.md); file and metadata checks are in the [public release verification record](../reference/publication-check.md).
